ConnectWise Cloud Backup supply chain compromise

Stolen vendor app credentials for ConnectWise (ex-SkyKick) multi-tenant M365 applications were used from October 15 to mint Graph tokens across ~2,250 tenants (Microsoft; Huntress counted 300+ successful of ~1,000 observed). Microsoft attributed the campaign to Storm-0940 on October 21, operating the ARTEX agentic-AI framework.

On Cutsign: brief story 10-19 Storm-0940 attribution 10-22 CISO card IoC cards news & updates (live)

Verdict: hunt now if the ConnectWise app IDs ever touched your tenant — including former customers. This is credential abuse of a vendor's multi-tenant apps, not a patchable CVE: there is nothing to scan for, only logs to review. Sophos reports the token sweep reached tenants with no active backup subscription, so past use counts. ConnectWise disabled the seven app identities, but previously issued tokens may remain usable until they expire, and attacker persistence (added credentials, role grants) survives the vendor-side fix.

30-second read

Password spraying (151,000+ failures, Oct 10–14) preceded abuse of stolen ConnectWise application credentials from Oct 15: service-principal sign-ins from attacker infrastructure minting Graph tokens in customer tenants. Follow-on activity where sign-ins succeeded: directory reconnaissance, new client secrets planted on other applications (persistence beyond the ConnectWise apps), and privileged role changes including a service principal self-assigning Global Administrator. Conditional Access policies scoped to users often do not evaluate multi-tenant service-principal sign-ins — check yours rather than assuming coverage.

Inferred detections — no Sentinel? same hunt, generic

The published queries above come first — Microsoft's KQL and the ConnectWise guide checklist supersede everything here. This is the fallback translation for environments without Defender XDR / Sentinel tables, over exported Entra logs in any SIEM or even a spreadsheet:

Service-principal sign-ins — the core question, tool-agnostic:

# exported Entra SP sign-in logs, Oct 1 → now
appId IN (the seven appIds above)
AND NOT (source = Azure private IPv6 AND user_agent = kiota-dotnet)
→ review every remaining row; Python/curl UAs from VPS space is the campaign

Persistence audit — a service principal doing admin things is the tell:

# Entra audit log, Oct 13 → now
operation IN ("Add service principal credentials", "Add member to role",
  "Update application", "Consent to application")
AND initiated_by is a service principal (not a user)
→ every hit gets a human review; self-role-assignment = incident

Network fallback — if Entra retention fails you, the infrastructure is still checkable:

# firewall / proxy / flow logs
dest IN (144.172.64.60, 144.172.64.99, 144.172.64.128, 144.172.94.243)
→ any match from identity infrastructure or egress since Oct 1

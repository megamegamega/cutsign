Attackers exploited CVE-2025-61624 in FortiOS to write files via the CLI and deploy the PEERSTITCH implant at a telecom managed service provider, according to researchers who analysed the intrusion in detail over several weeks.

The implant runs in memory, hooks the management daemon, and beacons over HTTPS to attacker infrastructure every few minutes, blending with legitimate FortiGuard update traffic to avoid detection by network monitoring.

Researchers recommend reviewing admin CLI sessions that write or delete files, and checking for unexpected processes spawned by the httpsd daemon on affected devices running vulnerable firmware.
